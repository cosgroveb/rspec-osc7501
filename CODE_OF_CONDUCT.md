# Code of conduct

Everyone who participates in good faith is welcome in rspec-osc7501. Treat
people with respect, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical
characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex, gender identity or expression,
sexual orientation, language, philosophy, religion, national or social origin,
income, education, or other status.

## Working together

Respect the project's purpose and other people's time. Discuss ideas without
attacking people. Make room for different viewpoints and experiences. Give and
accept constructive feedback. Take responsibility for your contributions and
for repairing harm you cause. Account for differences in language, culture,
and communication when interpreting what someone says.

Do not:

- Harass people, ignore a request to stop, or violate stated boundaries.
- Make personal attacks, discriminate, or stereotype people by identity.
- Make unwanted sexual comments or advances.
- Share or use someone's private information without permission.
- Threaten, encourage, or cause violence or other harm.
- Impersonate someone or evade moderation with another identity.
- Contribute someone else's work without credit.
- Post unrelated advertising or present harmful content without appropriate
  context and care.

Threatening or encouraging these behaviors also violates this policy. Other
behavior that threatens the community's well-being may warrant moderation.
Disagreement alone is not a conduct violation.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to Brian Cosgrove at
[cosgroveb@gmail.com](mailto:cosgroveb@gmail.com). Include what happened and any
relevant links or messages. Do not post private reports in public issues.

I review and investigate reports, hear from the people involved as needed, and
prioritize safety and confidentiality. I share report details only as needed
to investigate and respond. I handle enforcement privately and discuss any
public resolution with the people involved. I respond as availability permits,
without a guaranteed deadline.

Depending on severity and repetition, I may issue a warning, remove content,
restrict participation, or impose a temporary or permanent ban. Serious
violations may lead to an immediate ban. Where continued participation is
appropriate, I explain what needs to change and any conditions for returning.
These rules apply to maintainers as well as other participants.

## Scope

This policy covers project spaces and anyone officially representing the
project elsewhere, including through project accounts or at events.
